# Key Insights

This document summarizes the major findings identified from the Amazon sales dataset and Power BI dashboard.

## 1. Overall 2022 Performance

- Total 2022 sales were **$2.18M**.
- A total of **27,747 product records** were recorded in 2022.
- Total product reviews reached approximately **19.42M**.
- Q4 generated **$811.09K**, making it the strongest quarter of 2022.

## 2. Quarterly Sales Performance

| Quarter | Sales |
|---|---:|
| Q1 | $347.01K |
| Q2 | $448.34K |
| Q3 | $571.30K |
| Q4 | $811.09K |

Sales increased consistently across all four quarters.

Q4 sales were approximately **2.34 times Q1 sales**, indicating a substantial increase in sales activity toward the end of the year.

## 3. Monthly Sales Performance

The strongest monthly sales periods were:

- November: **$310.16K**
- December: **$293.27K**
- September: **$291.64K**
- October: **$207.66K**

September through December collectively represented a significant portion of annual sales, indicating strong late-year performance.

## 4. Product Category Performance

| Product Category | YTD Sales | % of YTD Sales |
|---|---:|---:|
| Men Shoes | $940.27K | 43.18% |
| Camera | $492.52K | 22.62% |
| Men Clothes | $357.64K | 16.42% |
| Car Accessories | $237.29K | 10.90% |
| Toys | $110.84K | 5.09% |
| Mobile & Accessories | $39.18K | 1.80% |

**Men Shoes** was the largest sales contributor, accounting for approximately **43.18%** of total 2022 sales.

Men Shoes and Camera together contributed approximately **65.80%** of total YTD sales.

## 5. Top Product Performance

The highest-selling products included:

- Nikon Wide Angle AF Nikkor 24mm f/2.8D Lens
- Atomos Ninja V 5-Inch 4K Photo and Video
- Solid Gear SGUS80006 Hydra GTX BOA Safety Shoe
- Canal Toys Photo Creator Instant Print Camera
- Vince Camuto Men's Lyre Dress Shoe Oxford

These products represent the leading individual revenue contributors within the selected 2022 dataset.

## 6. Customer Engagement

The dashboard also tracks product reviews as an indicator of customer engagement.

Products with high review counts can provide useful signals for:

- Customer interest
- Product visibility
- Product popularity
- Potential customer demand

However, review volume should not be interpreted as a direct measure of customer satisfaction without additional information such as review ratings or sentiment.

## 7. Business Recommendations

### Inventory Planning

Prioritize inventory planning for high-performing categories, particularly **Men Shoes** and **Camera**, to support demand during high-sales periods.

### Seasonal Planning

The significant increase in Q4 sales suggests that inventory and promotional planning should account for stronger demand during the later months of the year.

### Product-Level Analysis

Investigate the characteristics of top-selling products to identify patterns that may help improve product selection and merchandising strategies.

### Customer Engagement

Use review activity alongside sales performance to identify products with strong customer interest and investigate whether high engagement translates into higher sales.

### Category Optimization

Lower-performing categories should be analyzed further to identify opportunities related to pricing, product selection, marketing, and customer demand.

## Key Takeaway

The 2022 analysis shows a strong upward progression in quarterly sales, with **Q4 as the strongest quarter** and **Men Shoes as the largest product category by sales**.

The concentration of sales among a few major categories highlights the importance of effective inventory, merchandising, and seasonal planning.

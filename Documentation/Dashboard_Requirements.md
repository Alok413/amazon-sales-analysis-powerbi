# Dashboard Requirements

This document defines the visualization and filtering requirements for the Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard.

## Required Visualizations

### 1. YTD Sales by Month

**Chart Type:** Line Chart

**Purpose:**  
Visualize monthly sales trends throughout the year to identify seasonal patterns, growth trends, and significant changes in sales performance.

---

### 2. YTD Sales by Week

**Chart Type:** Column Chart

**Purpose:**  
Display weekly sales performance to identify short-term fluctuations and changes in sales activity.

---

### 3. Sales by Product Category

**Chart Type:** Table / Heat Map

**Required Metrics:**
- Product Category
- YTD Sales
- QTD Sales
- % YTD Sales

**Purpose:**  
Provide a high-level comparison of sales performance across different product categories.

---

### 4. Top 5 Products by YTD Sales

**Chart Type:** Bar Chart

**Purpose:**  
Identify the top-performing products based on year-to-date sales and highlight major revenue contributors.

---

### 5. Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews

**Chart Type:** Bar Chart

**Purpose:**  
Identify products with the highest number of year-to-date reviews and understand customer engagement.

---

## KPI Cards

The dashboard should display the following KPI cards:

- YTD Sales
- QTD Sales
- YTD Products Sold
- YTD Reviews

---

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

- Product Category
- Quarter

These filters allow users to dynamically explore sales performance based on selected categories and reporting periods.

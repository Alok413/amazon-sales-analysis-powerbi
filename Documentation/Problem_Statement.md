# Problem Statement

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Amazon Products Sales Analysis dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard is designed to monitor sales performance, product movement, customer engagement, and product-level performance using key business KPIs and visualizations.

## KPI Requirements

### YTD Sales

Monitor year-to-date sales to evaluate overall revenue performance over time.

### QTD Sales

Track quarterly sales performance to identify sales trends and fluctuations.

### YTD Products Sold

Analyze the total number of products sold throughout the year to understand product movement.

### YTD Reviews

Monitor year-to-date product reviews to understand customer engagement and feedback.

## Business Questions

The dashboard should help answer:

1. How are sales performing throughout the year?
2. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
3. Which products are the top revenue contributors?
4. Which products receive the highest number of reviews?
5. How does quarterly sales performance compare with yearly performance?
6. Which weeks and months show significant changes in sales?

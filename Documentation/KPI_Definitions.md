# KPI Definitions

This document defines the key performance indicators used in the Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard.

## 1. YTD Sales

**Definition:**  
Year-to-date sales represent the total sales generated from the beginning of the selected year through the selected reporting period.

**Dashboard Value (2022):** $2.18M

**Business Purpose:**  
Used to monitor overall revenue performance and understand how sales accumulate throughout the year.

---

## 2. QTD Sales

**Definition:**  
Quarter-to-date sales represent the total sales generated during the selected quarter.

**Dashboard Value (Q4 2022):** $811.09K

**Business Purpose:**  
Used to evaluate quarterly performance and identify significant changes or fluctuations in sales.

---

## 3. YTD Products Sold

**Definition:**  
YTD Products Sold represents the number of product records/orders recorded during the selected year.

**Dashboard Value (2022):** 27.75K

**Business Purpose:**  
Used to understand product movement and overall sales activity.

---

## 4. YTD Reviews

**Definition:**  
YTD Reviews represents the total number of product reviews associated with the selected year.

**Dashboard Value (2022):** 19.42M

**Business Purpose:**  
Used as an indicator of customer engagement and product interaction.

---

## KPI Summary

| KPI | 2022 Value | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| YTD Sales | $2.18M | Monitor annual sales performance |
| QTD Sales | $811.09K | Monitor quarterly sales performance |
| YTD Products Sold | 27.75K | Understand product movement |
| YTD Reviews | 19.42M | Measure customer engagement |
